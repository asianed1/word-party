# Word list

* `enable1.txt` - the **ENABLE** word list (Enhanced North American Benchmark LExicon, 172,823 words),
  which its authors placed in the **public domain**. Copy taken from
  https://github.com/dolph/dictionary (enable1.txt). Words of 2-15 letters are used.
* `extra-words.txt` - 11 common two-letter words that newer word-game lists accept but ENABLE predates
  (DA EW FE GI KI OI OK PO QI TE ZA). Added by this project; also public domain.
