      How to play (in 1 minute)

      1. Make words on the board with the 7 letter tiles on your rack, crossword-style.
      2. Place tiles: drag them onto the board, or tap a tile, then tap a square. Tap a placed tile to take it back.
      3. All new tiles go in one row or one column, with no gaps, and must join the tiles already there. The first word must cover the ★ centre.
      4. Score = the letter values of every word you make. Coloured squares boost it: 2× letter 3× letter 2× word 3× word. Use all 7 tiles in one turn for a +50 bonus!
      5. Blank tiles (no letter) can be any letter you pick, but score 0.
      6. Press Check to see if your words are valid and what they score, then Play. Stuck? Hint suggests a move, Swap trades tiles (uses your turn), or Pass.
      7. The end: when the bag is empty and someone uses their last tile - or after 6 turns in a row with no score. Leftover tiles count against you (and go to whoever went out first).

      Words are checked against the public-domain ENABLE word list.
